Instructor to TA

One button that lists every Blackboard course you are enrolled in as Instructor and lets you make yourself a Teaching Assistant, or leave, in the ones you tick.

Why this exists

Educational Designer access lets us self-enrol, and self-enrolling makes us an Instructor.

Instructors are now copied forward into next semester's course sites, so every course we have ever dropped into follows us into the future, sits on academics' dashboards, and confuses students about who is teaching. The request from the Blackboard team is to hold long-term access as a Teaching Assistant and to unenrol from courses we no longer need.

Doing that by hand means opening every course roster one at a time. This does it in one pass, and only for the courses you tick.

Before you worry about running a script

  • It changes nothing until you click a button, and then only in the courses you have ticked.
  • It touches only your own enrolment. It cannot change anyone else's role.
  • It only looks at courses where your role is exactly Instructor. Courses where you are already a TA, a student, or anything else are not listed.
  • It runs in your browser, signed in as you, and talks only to learn.uq.edu.au. Nothing is sent anywhere else and no outside code is loaded.
  • Both changes are reversible. Your Educational Designer access lets you re-enrol or upgrade yourself back at any time.
Easiest way

AUse the button

Set it up once and it becomes a button you press.

Drag the yellow bar below onto your bookmarks bar. If you cannot see your bookmarks bar, press Ctrl Shift B to show it.

Instructor to TA

Drag it up to the bar. Clicking it here does nothing.

Then open Blackboard, sign in, and click Instructor to TA in your bookmarks bar. A purple panel opens in the top right corner listing every course where you are an Instructor.

If no panel appears within a few seconds, your browser has blocked the bookmarklet. Use Option B instead.

What you will see

  1. The panel reads your enrolments. With a few hundred courses this takes about half a minute.
  2. Every course where you are an Instructor is listed with a tick box. All are ticked to start with. Untick any course you genuinely need to stay an Instructor in, for example one where you are building a Blackboard test or submitting grades this week.
  3. Click Make me a TA in ticked courses. The panel asks you to click once more to confirm, then works through the list. Or click Unenrol me from ticked to leave those courses altogether.
  4. Each course fades out as it is done. Reload Blackboard afterwards and your course list will show the new roles.

Teaching Assistant

Keeps you in the course with announcements, content, gradebook and marking. Everything except creating Blackboard tests and assignments and submitting final marks.

Unenrol

Removes you from the course. Use it for courses you only needed for a look. You can self-enrol again later.

Need Instructor again?

Open the course roster and change your own role back, do the job, then run this again.

If the button is not an option

BPaste it into the console

The console is a panel built into your browser where you can run a piece of code.

  1. Sign in to Blackboard in Chrome or Edge. Any Blackboard page will do.
  2. Press F12 and click the Console tab.
  3. The first time only, Chrome will ask you to type allow pasting and press enter before it lets you paste.
  4. Press the copy button below, click into the console, paste, and press enter.

Questions

Will this change anyone else's enrolment?

No. It only ever sends changes for your own user account, in the courses you ticked.

It says it could not read my enrolments

You are probably not signed in to Blackboard in that tab, or the session has expired. Sign in, load any Blackboard page, and click the bookmark again.

A course failed with HTTP 403

Blackboard refused the change for that course. Change your role on that course's roster by hand and tell Sean the course code.

I made myself a TA in a course I needed Instructor for

Open the course, go to the roster, and change your own role back to Instructor. Your Educational Designer access allows it.
